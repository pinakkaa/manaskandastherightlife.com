At Mana Skanda: Designed for Families. Built for The Right Life.

Mana Skanda The Right Life is a thoughtfully planned residential community in Sarjapur, Bengaluru, spread across 33 acres. With spacious homes, expansive open landscapes, and a wide range of family-focused amenities, the community is designed to bring comfort, connection, and a better way of living together.

From everyday recreation to spaces for children to learn, play, and grow, every aspect of the community is planned to create a vibrant and fulfilling lifestyle for the entire family.
